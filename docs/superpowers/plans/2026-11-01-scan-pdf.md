# Implementation Plan: Document Scanner & Perspective Warper (`/tools/scan-pdf`)

## Phase 1: Pure Engine & Unit Tests
- Implement `src/lib/pdf/scanEngine.ts`:
  - `computeHomography(srcQuad, dstWidth, dstHeight)`: Solves 8 linear equations via Gaussian elimination.
  - `warpPerspective(srcData, srcWidth, srcHeight, srcQuad, dstWidth, dstHeight)`: Bilinear inverse mapping.
  - `applyScanFilter(pixelData, filterType)`: 'bw' (adaptive/otsu threshold), 'grayscale', 'color-boost', 'none'.
  - `createScannedPdf(pages, pageSize)`: Embeds warped canvases/images into PDF pages via `pdf-lib`.
- Implement `tests/pdf/scanEngine.test.ts`:
  - Verify homography calculation produces expected identity or scaling coordinates.
  - Verify pixel warping on synthetic pixel arrays.
  - Verify multi-page PDF generation with specified page sizes.

## Phase 2: Markdown Content
- Create `src/content/tools/scan-pdf.md`:
  - Title: "Camera Document Scanner & Perspective Straightener to PDF"
  - Description: "Scan documents, receipts, whiteboards, and book pages with your phone or camera. Unskew angles with 4-point perspective warp and export high-contrast B&W PDFs 100% free."
  - How-to steps, features, FAQs (privacy, PACER/court acceptance, photo vs scanner quality).

## Phase 3: Astro Island Component
- Create `src/components/tools/ScanPdfIsland.astro`:
  - Live video camera capture (getUserMedia) with facingMode: "environment" (rear camera on phones).
  - Canvas with 4 interactive corner anchors (Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Right, Bottom-Left) and magnifying loupe or highlight border.
  - Filter preset chips (`Photocopy B&W`, `Clean Grayscale`, `Color Enhanced`, `Original`).
  - Page strip thumbnail manager.
  - Download Scanned PDF action.

## Phase 4: Route & Layout Wiring
