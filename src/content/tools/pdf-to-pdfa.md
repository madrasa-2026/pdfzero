### Core ISO Standards Supported

1. **PDF/A-2b (ISO 19005-2:2011 Level B — Recommended)**:
   The modern gold standard for digital archiving. Retains full support for vector transparencies, drop shadows, image layers, and modern compression while enforcing device-independent rendering.

2. **PDF/A-1b (ISO 19005-1:2005 Level B — Legacy Court Systems)**:
   The original 2005 standard required by conservative government portals, older court e-filing engines, and patent offices.

3. **PDF/A-3b (ISO 19005-3:2012 Level B — Electronic Invoicing)**:
   Allows structured electronic invoices (ZUGFeRD, Factur-X) and machine-readable XML datasets to be embedded within the archival document.
