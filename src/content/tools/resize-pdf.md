### Standardize and Re-Dimension PDF Pages Instantly

Different countries, courts, and corporate environments enforce strict paper dimension standards. For instance, European and Asian organizations standardise on **ISO A4 (210 × 297 mm)**, while North American businesses and courts demand **US Letter (8.5 × 11 in)** or **US Legal (8.5 × 14 in)**. Sending an incorrectly sized PDF often leads to cut-off edges, awkward print scaling, or rejection by automated filing portals.

Commercial software such as Adobe Acrobat DC or PDFfiller charges costly monthly subscriptions for basic page resizing. Free online converters often compress pages into blurry images, watermark the result, or leak sensitive data to remote servers.

### Dual Resizing Modes: Proportional Fit vs. Gutter Expansion

- **Proportional Fit (`fit`):** Automatically scales the content to fit comfortably within the target boundaries, maintaining aspect ratios without distortion. Add an optional safety margin (e.g. 15–30 pt) so content never touches the paper edge.
- **Canvas Gutter Expansion (`expand`):** Keeps original page content at exactly 100% scale while enlarging the canvas around the edges. Perfect for creating binding margins for spiral coils, 3-ring binders, or grading notes.
