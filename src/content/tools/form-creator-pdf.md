### Stop Printing and Scanning Static Paper Forms

Millions of important documents distributed every day—from lease agreements and medical questionnaires to vendor onboarding forms and school permissions—are saved as static, "flat" PDFs. Recipients are forced to print them out, scribble in the lines with a pen, scan them back in, or struggle with makeshift annotation overlays that misalign across different devices.

Software vendors like Adobe exploit this frustration by gatekeeping interactive form creation behind **Adobe Acrobat Pro** ($239/year), while online tools lock you out after creating a single test box.

AirPDF's **Interactive Form Creator & AcroForm Injector** brings enterprise-grade form building to your browser for free, with zero server uploads and zero privacy risks.
