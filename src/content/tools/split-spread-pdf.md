### Read Scanned Books, Textbooks & Sheet Music Comfortably on Any Screen

Two-page spreads are common in digital libraries, university archives, and sheet music collections. While scanning two facing pages at once saves time, reading them on modern devices—such as phones, tablets, or e-readers—is agonizing because you must constantly pinch-to-zoom, pan left, pan right, and scroll in zig-zags.

Commercial tools like Adobe Acrobat Pro ($239/year), Sejda, and PDF-XChange charge fees or impose strict daily page limits for spread cutting. Free alternatives watermark your pages or upload sensitive copyrighted books to third-party cloud servers.

AirPDF's **PDF Two-Page Spread Cutter** runs 100% client-side in your web browser, converting awkward side-by-side scans into smooth, single-page reading experiences in seconds.
