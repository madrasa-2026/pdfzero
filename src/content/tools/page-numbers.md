### Professional Document Pagination Made Simple

Organized page numbering is essential for academic dissertations, legal court filings, financial audits, and enterprise manuals. When distributing multi-page documents to clients or review committees, referencing specific page numbers during conference calls or in formal citations requires clear, consistent pagination.

PDFZero allows you to stamp elegant, uniform page numbers directly onto existing PDF files in seconds. You don't need complex desktop publishing software like Adobe InDesign or Acrobat Pro to achieve crisp, publication-grade results.

### Full Control Over Placement and Formatting

Our interactive pagination settings give you complete aesthetic and structural authority over your document:
- **6-Way Position Grid:** Place numbers in the bottom-right footer for standard reports, bottom-center for academic essays, or top-right header for business correspondence.
- **Dynamic Syntax:** Choose between standard cardinal numbers (1, 2, 3), descriptive phrasing (Page 1 of 24), or concise ratios (1 / 24).
- **Cover Page Intelligence:** Prevent unsightly numbers on title sheets or graphical cover artwork by toggling our cover page exclusion switch.

### 100% Client-Side Vector Typography

Page numbers are rendered using high-resolution embedded Helvetica font vectors directly inside your browser memory. This guarantees ultra-sharp text reproduction on high-DPI screens and industrial laser printers without bloating your file size.
