### Lock Fillable Forms into Permanent, Tamper-Proof Documents

Interactive PDF forms (AcroForms) with fillable boxes, checkboxes, and signature fields are ideal for gathering data. However, once a form is filled and ready for official submission, leaving it interactive is a major vulnerability:

1. **Inconsistent Rendering:** Many mobile readers, web viewers, and court e-filing systems fail to render interactive fields, displaying submitted forms as completely blank.
2. **Tampering & Accidental Edits:** Unflattened forms allow any recipient to alter numbers, modify terms, or uncheck boxes.
3. **Printing Glitches:** Certain office printers skip interactive form layers entirely.
