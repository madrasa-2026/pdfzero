### Core Prepress Standards Supported

1. **8 Corner Trim Marks**:
   Precision 0.35 pt hairline ticks indicating exact physical knife cut coordinates, offset by 4 pt from artwork corners to eliminate stray marks on the final cut product.

2. **Bleed & Trim Box Dictionaries**:
   Applies native `/TrimBox` and `/BleedBox` metadata so prepress RIP (Raster Image Processor) software detects your exact print geometry automatically.

3. **4-Point Registration Bullseyes**:
   Concentric alignment crosshairs positioned along the top, bottom, left, and right slug centers for 4-color plate alignment.

4. **10-Step CMYK Densitometer Color Bars**:
   Standard spectrophotometer calibration strips for Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Black ink saturation monitoring.

5. **Production Slug Line**:
   Stamps document filename, page number sequence, trim dimensions in millimeters, and output timestamp directly in the slug margin.
