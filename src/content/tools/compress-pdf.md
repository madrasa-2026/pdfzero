### Why Use In-Browser PDF Compression?

Large PDF files are a frequent obstacle when sending email attachments, submitting online university applications, or filing official tax and legal forms. Many email providers restrict incoming attachments to 20MB or 25MB, while institutional portals often enforce strict limits as low as 2MB or 5MB. 

AirPDF provides a fast, zero-install solution that shrinks bloated documents right inside your browser window. By converting dense, unoptimized scanned pages into highly tuned visual assets, you can achieve drastic reductions in total file weight within seconds.

### Transparent Image-Based Optimization

Unlike native desktop vector processors that cost hundreds of dollars, our free web tool operates by rendering individual page layouts into optimized canvas graphics. This technique delivers extraordinary size reductions on scanned contracts, receipts, presentations, and picture-heavy brochures. Please keep in mind that since pages are re-encoded as high-efficiency images, embedded text strings will no longer be highlighted or searched via text search commands.

### Tips for Optimal Compression Results

- **For Standard Documents:** Select the **Recommended** preset (150 DPI) for a balanced ratio between crisp readability and compact file size.
- **For Government Forms:** When strict submission caps apply (e.g. under 1MB), use the **Extreme** preset to strip maximum overhead.
- **For High-Fidelity Artwork:** Choose **Less Compression** (300 DPI) to preserve sharp print typography and technical diagrams.
