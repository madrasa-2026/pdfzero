Unlike visual diff tools that get confused by slight margin adjustments, AirPDF extracts the textual data stream:

- **Crimson Red Deletions (-):** Removed terms or dropped liability protections from Document A are displayed with red strike-through markings.
- **Similarity Score:** An instant match percentage indicates how closely the two revisions align.
