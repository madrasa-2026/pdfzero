### Precision Mathematical Page Ordering

Folding a stack of paper requires non-linear page ordering:

- **Sheet 1 (Exterior Cover):** Prints the back cover (Page $N$) on the left and the front cover (Page 1) on the right.
- **Sheet 1 (Inside Spread):** Prints Page 2 on the left and Page $N-1$ on the right.
- **Sheet 2 (Inner Spread):** Prints Page $N-2$ on the left and Page 3 on the right.
- **Automatic Multiples of 4 Padding:** Documents with non-divisible page counts are padded with clean, non-intrusive blank backing pages.

### Easy Printing Instructions
1. Open the downloaded PDF in your preferred PDF reader (Chrome, Preview, Adobe Acrobat Reader).
2. Choose **Print**.
3. Select **Two-Sided (Duplex)** printing.
4. Set duplex binding to **Flip on Short Edge** (Landscape tumble).
5. Fold your printed stack down the center line and staple across the spine!
