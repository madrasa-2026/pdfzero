### Stop Silent Privacy Leaks in PDF Documents

Every PDF document you create carries hidden forensic fingerprints. When you draft a contract in Word, design a pitch deck in Keynote, or export an architectural schematic from CAD, your software silently embeds invisible metadata:
- **Author Identity:** Your full name, computer user profile, and department.
- **Operating Environment:** Your computer hostname, operating system version, and exact PDF producer software.
- **Forensic Timestamps:** The exact second the document was authored, edited, and exported.
- **XMP Tracking Streams:** Extensible XML metadata packets containing digital asset IDs, prior filenames, and editing software history.

Sharing unscrubbed documents can be disastrous in legal disputes, investigative journalism, corporate negotiations, and double-blind academic reviews.
